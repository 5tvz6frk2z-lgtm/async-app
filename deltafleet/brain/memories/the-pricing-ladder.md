# The pricing ladder

Affordability at the bottom, moat at the top. The four rungs:

1. **Daily Brief** — $99 setup + $79/month (or $49/month bring-your-own-API-key). A read-only starter agent; setup is credited toward a Recon.
2. **Recon** — a paid workflow audit (~$2k) that produces a fixed-price plan; the fee is credited from the starter setup.
3. **Install** — $8,000–$20,000 fixed, one to three corridors live in 30 days.
4. **Flight Ops** — a managed retainer with a monthly Delta Proof report.

Legacy marketing retainer tiers (Scout / Squadron / Armada) still apply to the marketing corridors.
