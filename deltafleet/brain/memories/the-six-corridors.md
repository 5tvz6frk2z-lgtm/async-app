# The six corridors

A corridor is one installable workflow, fully described by a blueprint. The six:

1. **Speed-to-Lead** — respond to inbound leads in minutes.
2. **Inbox & CRM Hygiene** — triage, label, keep the CRM clean.
3. **Reporting Autopilot** — weekly reports, numbers computed not generated.
4. **Content / AEO Engine** — answer-engine-optimized content.
5. **Document Intake** — invoices and documents matched and posted.
6. **Review & Reputation** — draft public responses to reviews.

Plus the read-only **Daily Brief** starter (inbox + calendar, debriefed every weekday).
